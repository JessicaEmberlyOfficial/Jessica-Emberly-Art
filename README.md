## About
Jessica Emberly is an american artist from Kansas. Jessica uses her art as an escape from the world.

She learned about her favorite horror film at a young age, and by the film being SAW (2004), She started fantasizing about unaliving what she deemed aweful people, and to ease that, she lives out her fantasies through her art.
